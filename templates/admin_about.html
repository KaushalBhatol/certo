What is Certo?

Certo is a self-hosted SSL Certificate Authority management tool. It lets you create Root CAs, issue SSL certificates signed by those CAs, and manage the full certificate lifecycle — including import, export, and reissue.

Designed for internal infrastructure, Certo runs fully air-gapped with no external CDN or internet dependencies, making it ideal for secure, isolated environments.
